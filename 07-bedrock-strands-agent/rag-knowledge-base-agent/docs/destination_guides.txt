Horizon Travel — Destination Guides (2026)

BARCELONA, SPAIN
Highlights: Sagrada Familia, Park Güell, Gothic Quarter, La Rambla. Best Apr-Jun, Sep-Oct. Tapas in El Born, beach at Barceloneta. Metro T-casual 10 trips €11. Book Sagrada Familia 2 weeks ahead.

TOKYO, JAPAN
Highlights: Shibuya Crossing (world's busiest), Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, Tsukiji Outer Market seafood, Meiji Shrine, teamLab Borderless. Suica/PASMO essential for metro. Cherry blossom late March, autumn colors November. Day trip to Hakone for Mt Fuji views.

ROME, ITALY
Highlights: Colosseum, Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel, Trevi Fountain, Trastevere food. Best Mar-May, Sep-Nov. Roma Pass 72h €52. Book Colosseum timed entry 1 week ahead. Try carbonara and supplì.

DUBAI, UAE
Highlights: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall & Fountain, Desert safari, Gold Souk, Palm Jumeirah. Best Nov-Mar (25-30°C). Metro day pass AED 20. Friday brunch tradition. Dress modestly in old Dubai.

SYDNEY, AUSTRALIA
Highlights: Opera House, Harbour Bridge Climb, Bondi Beach, Blue Mountains day trip. Best Sep-Nov, Mar-May. Opal card for transport. Coastal walk Bondi to Coogee 6km.

REYKJAVIK, ICELAND
Highlights: Golden Circle (Thingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss), Blue Lagoon ($80 entry, book 3 weeks ahead), Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon & Diamond Beach, Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach (sneaker waves). Ring Road 7-10 days, 4x4 for F-roads. Northern Lights Sep-Mar, check vedur.is.

NEW YORK, USA
Highlights: Times Square, Central Park, Statue of Liberty ferry, Broadway, High Line. Best Apr-Jun, Sep-Nov. Subway 7-day $33. Book Broadway lottery day-of. Bagels and pizza essentials.

CAPE TOWN, SOUTH AFRICA
Highlights: Table Mountain cableway, Robben Island, Cape Point, Kirstenbosch Gardens, Winelands Stellenbosch. Best Nov-Mar. MyCiti bus card. Chapman's Peak Drive scenic. Penguin colony at Boulders Beach.

KYOTO, JAPAN
Highlights: Fushimi Inari 10,000 torii gates, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (early morning), Kiyomizu-dera Temple ¥400, Gion geisha district. PASMO/ICOCA day pass ¥700. Kaiseki dining, Golden Week Apr 29-May 5 peak.
