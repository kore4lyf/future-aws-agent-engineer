Horizon Travel — Travel Policies (v2026-03)

1. FARE CLASSES
- Economy Flex: Changeable, 25% cancellation 24h-7d, 50% 7d-48h, 100% <48h. 1x23kg included.
- Economy Saver: Non-changeable, non-refundable after 24h. 1x23kg included.
- Premium Economy: Changeable with $75 fee. 2x23kg included.
- Business: Fully changeable, refundable. 2x32kg included.
- All fares: Full refund within 24h if departure >7 days away (Terms §4).

2. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY (Terms §4)
- Within 24 hours of booking: Full refund, no fee — provided departure is >7 days away.
- 24 hours to 7 days before departure: 25% cancellation fee per passenger.
- 7 days to 48 hours before departure: 50% cancellation fee per passenger.
- Less than 48 hours before departure: 100% forfeiture, no refund.
- No-show without prior cancellation: 100% of fare plus $50 processing fee.
- Refunds processed within 7-10 business days to original payment method.
- Weather or Horizon-initiated cancellations qualify for full refund or free rebooking under Policy §9.

3. BAGGAGE POLICY (IATA §3.2)
Cabin baggage: 1 carry-on ≤7kg, max 55x35x25cm. Included in all fares.
Checked baggage:
- Economy: 1 x 23kg included; extra bag $60 per segment.
- Premium Economy: 2 x 23kg included; extra bag $60.
- Business: 2 x 32kg included; extra bag $80.
Overweight / Oversize:
- 23-32kg overweight fee: $40 per bag (Economy/Premium). Business included up to 32kg.
- >32kg not accepted; must split or ship cargo.
- Oversize (>158cm linear): $75 per bag.
Restricted items: Lithium batteries >100Wh, flammables, sharp objects prohibited in checked baggage.
Sports equipment (bicycles, surfboards, ski sets): $50 flat fee if within 23kg, otherwise overweight fees apply.

4. TRAVEL INSURANCE (Standard Cover)
Standard travel insurance covers:
- Medical emergencies abroad up to $100,000 including hospitalisation and repatriation.
- Trip cancellation/interruption due to covered reasons (illness, severe weather) up to booking value.
- Baggage loss/delay: $1,500 per bag, $300 for delay >6 hours.
- 24/7 assistance hotline.
Exclusions: Pre-existing conditions without declaration, extreme sports without add-on, travel against government advice.
Premium Cover adds: $250,000 medical, adventure sports, cancel-for-any-reason 50% refund, and gadget cover $2,000.

5. LOYALTY PROGRAMME (Horizon Points)
- Earn 1 point per $1 spent, double points on Business.
- Points value: 100 points = $1 off future bookings.
- Tiers: Silver (5k points, free seat selection), Gold (15k, lounge access), Platinum (30k, free extra bag + priority boarding).
- Points expire after 24 months of inactivity.

6. GENERAL
All cancellations must be requested via the portal or support email. Quote clause §4 when advising customers.
