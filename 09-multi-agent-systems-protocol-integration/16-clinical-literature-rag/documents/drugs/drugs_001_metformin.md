# Metformin: First-Line Type 2 Diabetes Therapy

Metformin lowers hepatic glucose production and improves insulin sensitivity. It is the preferred initial pharmacotherapy for most adults with type 2 diabetes unless contraindicated. Common adverse effects are gastrointestinal; rare lactic acidosis risk rises with severe renal impairment.

Long-term outcomes data support cardiovascular safety. Dose titration minimizes GI intolerance. Monitoring includes renal function, vitamin B12 with prolonged use, and periodic HbA1c assessment.
