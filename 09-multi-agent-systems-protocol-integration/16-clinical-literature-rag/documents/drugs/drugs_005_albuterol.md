# Albuterol: Short-Acting Beta-2 Agonist

Albuterol relieves acute bronchospasm in asthma and COPD within minutes. It is the rescue inhaler of choice; increased rescue use signals poor underlying control. Tremor and tachycardia are dose-related sympathomimetic effects.

Over-reliance without controller therapy worsens outcomes. Device technique and spacer use improve lung deposition. Severe exacerbations may require systemic corticosteroids and oxygen beyond bronchodilators alone.
