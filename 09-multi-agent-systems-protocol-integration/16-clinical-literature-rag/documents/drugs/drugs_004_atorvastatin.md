# Atorvastatin: HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor

Atorvastatin reduces LDL cholesterol by upregulating hepatic LDL receptors. It is indicated for dyslipidemia and cardiovascular risk reduction. Myalgia is the most common intolerance; rhabdomyolysis is rare and dose-related.

Interactions include CYP3A4 inhibitors and grapefruit juice. Baseline and follow-up lipid panels guide titration. Pregnancy categorizes statins as generally avoided unless benefits clearly outweigh risks.
