# Sertraline: Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor

Sertraline is an SSRI indicated for major depressive disorder, OCD, panic disorder, and PTSD. Onset of mood improvement takes 2–4 weeks; sleep and anxiety may change earlier. Sexual dysfunction, nausea, and activation are common early effects.

Discontinuation syndrome is minimized by gradual taper. Serotonin syndrome risk rises with other serotonergic agents. Monitoring includes clinical response, suicidality in young adults, and hyponatremia in older adults.
