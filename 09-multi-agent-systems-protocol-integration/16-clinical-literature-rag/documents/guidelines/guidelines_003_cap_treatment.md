# Community-Acquired Pneumonia Treatment

Outpatient CAP in healthy adults may use amoxicillin or doxycycline; comorbidities broaden coverage to respiratory fluoroquinolones or beta-lactam plus macrolide. Severity scores guide inpatient versus outpatient disposition.

Reassess at 48–72 hours for clinical response. Cultures and biomarkers inform de-escalation when atypical or resistant pathogens are suspected. Vaccination reduces recurrence risk in eligible populations.
