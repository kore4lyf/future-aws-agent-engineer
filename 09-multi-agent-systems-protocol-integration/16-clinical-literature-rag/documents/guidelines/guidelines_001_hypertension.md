# Hypertension Diagnosis and Initial Management

Adults with confirmed hypertension receive lifestyle counseling and, when indicated, pharmacotherapy based on cardiovascular risk. Home and ambulatory monitoring confirm white-coat phenomena. First-line classes include ACE inhibitors/ARBs, calcium channel blockers, and thiazide-like diuretics.

Follow-up intervals reflect control status and drug titration needs. Resistant hypertension triggers adherence review, secondary-cause screening, and mineralocorticoid antagonist consideration.
