# Type 2 Diabetes Glycemic Management

A1C targets are individualized; many non-pregnant adults aim for below 7%. Metformin remains foundational lifestyle plus first pharmacotherapy. Add-on options consider comorbidities: SGLT2 inhibitors for heart failure or CKD, GLP-1 receptor agonists for obesity or ASCVD.

Self-management education, nutrition, activity, and sleep are integral. Sequential therapy intensification avoids prolonged hyperglycemia while monitoring for hypoglycemia and weight effects.
