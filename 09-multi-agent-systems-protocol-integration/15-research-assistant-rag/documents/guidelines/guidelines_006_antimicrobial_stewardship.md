# Antimicrobial Stewardship Principles

Empiric antibiotics should cover likely pathogens using the narrowest effective spectrum, then de-escalate with culture data. Duration is the shortest evidence-based course that clears infection. Prospective audit and feedback reduce unnecessary broad-spectrum days.

Documented allergies, prior cultures, and local antibiograms guide selection. Stewardship programs track DOT, C. difficile rates, and resistance trends to close the feedback loop with prescribers.
