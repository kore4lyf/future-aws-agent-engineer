# Asthma Exacerbation and Controller Step Therapy

Stepwise controller therapy matches symptom frequency and lung function. Inhaled corticosteroids form the backbone; LABA is added at higher steps. Rescue SABA use more than twice weekly prompts controller review.

Exacerbation plans include short-course oral corticosteroids, technique review, and trigger reduction. Biologics and add-on agents are reserved for severe uncontrolled disease after adherence and comorbidity optimization.
