# Amoxicillin-Clavulanate: Beta-Lactam with Beta-Lactamase Inhibitor

Amoxicillin-clavulanate covers common respiratory and soft-tissue pathogens, including beta-lactamase producers. Clavulanate protects amoxicillin from hydrolysis by many extended-spectrum enzymes. Typical uses include acute bacterial sinusitis, otitis media, and community-acquired pneumonia.

Diarrhea is the most frequent adverse effect; taking food reduces GI upset. Dosing adjusts for renal function and weight in pediatric patients. Unnecessary courses promote resistance and should be avoided.
