# Lisinopril: ACE Inhibitor Overview

Lisinopril inhibits angiotensin-converting enzyme to reduce blood pressure and afterload. Indications include hypertension, heart failure, and post-myocardial infarction management. Dry cough and hyperkalemia are class effects; angioedema is rare but serious.

Contraindications include pregnancy and bilateral renal artery stenosis. Combination with potassium-sparing diuretics requires electrolyte monitoring. Titration targets guideline blood-pressure goals.
