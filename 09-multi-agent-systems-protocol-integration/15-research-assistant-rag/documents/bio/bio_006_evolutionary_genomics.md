# Evolutionary Genomics and Phylogenetics

Sequence divergence, recombination, and selection shape genome evolution over time. Phylogenetic trees inferred from sequence alignments reconstruct ancestry and date divergence events using molecular clocks.

Comparative genomics identifies conserved elements under purifying selection and lineage-specific innovations. Population genetic statistics—Tajima's D, F_ST, haplotype diversity—reveal demography and local adaptation in natural populations.
