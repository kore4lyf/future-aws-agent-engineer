# CRISPR-Cas Gene Editing

CRISPR-Cas systems use guide RNAs to direct nuclease activity to complementary DNA loci. Off-target cleavage is reduced by high-fidelity Cas variants, truncated guides, and careful computational guide design. Base editors and prime editors enable nucleotide changes without double-strand breaks.

Applications span functional genomics screens, agricultural trait improvement, and therapeutic development for monogenic disorders. Delivery constraints—viral vectors versus ribonucleoprotein complexes—shape in vivo efficacy and immunogenicity profiles.
