# Single-Cell RNA Sequencing

Single-cell transcriptomics measures gene expression per cell, revealing rare cell types and differentiation trajectories. Droplet microfluidics and plate-based platforms capture thousands to millions of cells per run. Count matrices are normalized, batch-corrected, and clustered before marker-gene interpretation.

Trajectory inference and RNA velocity reconstruct developmental time. Multi-modal extensions pair transcriptomes with surface proteins or chromatin accessibility for richer cell-state definitions.
