# Microbiome and Host Health

The human microbiome influences immunity, metabolism, and neuroendocrine signaling. 16S rRNA and shotgun metagenomics profile community composition; metatranscriptomics captures active pathways. Dysbiosis associations exist for inflammatory bowel disease, obesity, and antibiotic-associated complications.

Causal inference remains challenging: interventions such as fecal microbiota transplantation and defined consortia test whether community shifts drive phenotype or follow it.
