# Reinforcement Learning Basics

Agent-environment loops optimize a cumulative reward signal through policy exploration. Q-learning estimates action values offline, while policy-gradient methods optimize the policy directly. Exploration strategies such as epsilon-greedy and entropy regularization balance try-new versus exploit-known behavior.

Deep reinforcement learning applies function approximation to high-dimensional state spaces. Stability techniques include experience replay, target networks, and clipped surrogate objectives in proximal policy optimization.
