# Machine Learning for Scientific Discovery

Supervised and unsupervised learning accelerate hypothesis generation in the natural sciences. Feature learning from high-throughput instruments reduces manual expert encoding. Active learning selects the most informative experiments under budget constraints.

In genomics, models predict regulatory activity from DNA sequence, infer cell types from single-cell RNA profiles, and prioritize variants for experimental follow-up. Protein structure predictors such as AlphaFold demonstrate how representation learning compresses decades of structural biology knowledge into transferable embeddings.
